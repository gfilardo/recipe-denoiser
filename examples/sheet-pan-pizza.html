Sheet-pan pizza made with a spoon

Keep the oil quantities separate. Dough oil: 30 g. Topping oil: 25 g total, shared between mixing and drizzling. Pan oil: as needed. The source says “about half” and “a little more”; no exact gram split has been invented.

Cooking plan

The topping can be mixed while the dough rests. The mozzarella can drain during the first bake.
